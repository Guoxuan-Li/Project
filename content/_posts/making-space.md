---
title: Making Space for Unfinished Things
date: 2026-06-04
tags: [essay, creative-work]
description: A note about drafts, patience, and leaving a little room around an idea.
---

An unfinished thing is not always a failed thing. Sometimes it is an idea that
has not yet found the shape capable of holding it.

I have started leaving drafts visible: a paragraph on the desk, a sketch beside
the kettle, a title pinned where I pass it every morning. Proximity does some of
the work that discipline cannot.
