# France

## Paris (5d)

The first morning was disorienting. The light was different, the bread was better, and every street felt like it belonged in a film I had already seen.

We walked for hours, got lost near the river, and found a small cafe that served the simplest and best lunch of the trip.

# Japan

## Tokyo (7d)

A city that rewards aimless wandering. Every turn revealed something -- a tiny shrine, a vending machine selling something unexpected, a side street that felt impossibly calm.

The trains were exact. The convenience stores were better than they had any right to be.

## Kyoto (3d)

Slower than Tokyo. Temples in the morning, tea in the afternoon, and a quiet walk along the river at night.

# Italy

## Rome (4d)

Layers of history stacked on top of each other. You turn a corner and two thousand years stare back at you.

The pasta was as good as everyone says.

## Florence (2d)

Compact, walkable, and saturated with art. The light on the river at sunset was unreal.
