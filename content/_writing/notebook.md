<span class="writing-work__title">A Quiet Morning</span>
</summary>
<div class="writing-work__body" markdown="1">

The kettle clicked off. Outside, the street was still dark, and the only sound was the rain against the window.

I poured the water, waited, and poured again. The ritual was the same as every morning, but today it felt different -- like the first page of a book I had not yet read.

### One

The walk to the station took longer than usual. I did not mind.

### Two

On the train, I watched the city wake up. Lights in windows, smoke from chimneys, a dog on a balcony.
