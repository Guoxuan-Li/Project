# Simple Tomato Pasta

- [From Classic Italian]

A quick weeknight dinner that takes about 15 minutes from start to finish.

## Ingredients

- 200g pasta
- 400g canned tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- Fresh basil

## Steps

1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.
2. Meanwhile, gently fry garlic in olive oil until fragrant.
3. Add tomatoes, simmer 8-10 minutes.
4. Toss with drained pasta, season, and top with basil.

# Five-Minute Egg

- [From Morning Routine]
