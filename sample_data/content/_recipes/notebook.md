# Lemon Butter Pasta

- [From Weeknight Notes]

## Ingredients

- Spaghetti
- One lemon
- Butter and parmesan
- Black pepper

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta and reserve a cup of water.
2. Emulsify butter, lemon zest, cheese, and pasta water.
3. Toss, season, and finish with lemon juice.
